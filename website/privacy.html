Privacy Policy

Lovely Home — home hub for your wall tablet.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Overview

Lovely Home is a progressive web app operated for private household use. Each installation runs on infrastructure chosen by that household (for example Cloudflare Pages and Workers). This policy describes how the product handles information in general.

This marketing site at lovely-home.co.uk is static — it does not collect analytics or account data. If you email support, we use your message only to respond.

Household hub data

On a running hub, typical data includes:

  • House guide content — Topics, photos, and setup details you enter as the owner.
  • Hub profile — Contacts, bin schedule, pet care notes, and display preferences you configure.
  • Secrets — Wi‑Fi details, integration tokens, and similar values stored encrypted server-side.
  • Session information — Device mode (owner vs sitter) and access cookies to keep sitter mode working between visits.

That data is stored in the household’s own Cloudflare account (D1, R2, secrets) and is not shared with other Lovely Home installations.

Third-party services

A household hub may call external services configured by the owner, such as:

  • Weather providers for forecast display
  • Alexa Virtual Buttons (or similar) for routine triggers
  • Calendar feeds for “My Day” (owner mode)
  • Cloudflare Access for login gating

Those services receive only what is needed for the feature the owner enabled.

Local storage

The hub may cache guide content and preferences in the browser for speed and offline resilience. Display preferences are also synced to the household profile so multiple tablets stay consistent.
