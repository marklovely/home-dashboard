Information at the right time

Give people what they need, when they need it. You do not have to permanently share Wi‑Fi, access details, or private house information — schedule access around the stay and let Lovely Home handle the timing.

Before arrival

Useful preparation

They sign in and read the house guide — heating, appliances, bins, and emergency contacts. Wi‑Fi, address, and lockbox stay hidden until the stay.

During the stay

Relevant details unlock

Home-access information appears automatically on the stay dates — no extra toggle for a booked stay.

After departure

Access expires

Their login is removed the day after the stay ends. You can end a stay early in Settings → House sitter mode.
