The right information. At the right time.

Give people what they need, when they need it. Schedule a stay in advance and control when information becomes available — so guests and sitters can prepare before they arrive, get what they need while they are there, and lose access when the stay ends.

Before arrival

Prepare without oversharing

They can sign in and read the guide — heating, appliances, emergency contacts, and everyday household information. Wi‑Fi, address, and lockbox stay hidden until the stay begins.

During the stay

What they need, when it matters

Home-access information appears automatically on the stay dates — no extra step for a booked stay.

After departure

Access ends with the stay

Their login is removed the day after checkout. You can end a stay early in Settings → House sitter mode.

Schedule stays with dates and an email in Settings → House sitter mode. How to set it up.
