No hardware required

Set it up

Your hub is a private web address. Send it to a sitter on their phone, or open it on a wall tablet if you have one. Use the 7-day trial to fill in the house guide — not a complimentary stay.

What you need

  • A browser — phone, laptop, or tablet. Sitters use the same hub URL you do. Nothing to buy.
  • About 30 minutes for the setup wizard once your hub is live (usually around 10 minutes after checkout).
  • Optional: a wall tablet — only if you want a screen in the house. We do not sell mounts, tablets, or kiosk hardware.

After you start a trial

  1. Wait for the hub. The success page watches the build and shows an Open button plus a QR code when it answers — usually around 10 minutes.
  2. Sign in as owner. Open {your-name}.lovely-hub.com. Cloudflare emails a one-time code to the address you used at signup.
  3. Walk through the wizard. Hub name, contacts, Wi‑Fi, lockbox, bins, optional calendar, and a starter house guide.
  4. Set an owner PIN. That unlocks owner tools on any device — phone, laptop, or tablet.
  5. Share the URL. Send the hub address to your sitter, or open it yourself wherever you like.

Send it to a sitter

This is the usual way. They open the hub on their own phone or laptop — no wall tablet required.

  1. Scheduled stay (usual). Settings → House sitter mode → add their email and dates. They can sign in from a few days before; Wi‑Fi and lockbox show on the stay dates; access drops after the stay ends.
  2. When the sit starts. Turn on Sitter is here so home-access details appear for that stay.
  3. Long-term sitter. Add their email as a permanent sitter login — not for one-off visits.

Sitters never see the guide editor, backups, or home-control buttons. How access is locked down.

Optional: wall tablet

If you want the hub on the wall as well, open the same URL on a tablet and add it to the home screen. Android users who want a locked, always-on display can use Fully Kiosk Browser — a separate app with its own licence, not part of Lovely Home.

  1. Install Fully and turn on Remote Admin (local network) with a password before you lock kiosk mode.
  2. Set the start URL to your hub, for example https://rose-cottage.lovely-hub.com.
  3. Keep cookies. If Fully clears cookies on reload, Cloudflare will ask for a login code every time the screen wakes.
  4. Choose an exit gesture and kiosk PIN, write them down, then enable kiosk mode.
  5. On the hub, enable House sitter mode on that device before you mount it.

Prefer not to use Fully? Chrome or Safari “Add to Home Screen” is enough for a simpler setup.

Stuck?

Email support@lovely-home.co.uk with your hub address. If you use a kiosk PIN on a tablet, we cannot reset it — keep that with your household notes.

Use the week to fill in the guide before anyone stays. A wall tablet is optional.
