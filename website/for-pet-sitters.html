For pet owners

Everything your pet sitter needs at home

When you travel, your pet sitter needs more than feeding notes in a message. Lovely Home keeps pet routines, house instructions, bins, and weather on one private link they can open on their phone — the same hub works for Airbnb guests, tenants, and family too.

What to put in the house guide

  • Feeding times, portions, and where food and treats live
  • Walk schedule, harness and lead location, and garden rules
  • Vet and emergency contacts, plus medication instructions
  • Alarm codes, heating, and anything unusual about your home
  • Bin days — easy to forget when someone else is in the house

Photos and search make it easy for a new sitter to find the right cupboard or switch.

Schedule the stay in advance

Add their email and dates under Settings → House sitter mode. They get the guide two weeks before the stay so they can prepare — Wi‑Fi, address, and lockbox appear only during the stay dates.

Have a regular dog walker or cat sitter? Add them as a permanent guest login, or book each visit in the same list.

Calm handover, private hub

Each household gets its own address at your-name.lovely-hub.com. Only emails you allow can sign in. Guests use the browser on their phone — no app store download.
