How billing works

  1. Free plan — create your home at /signup. No charge for one home with up to two guides and two scheduled stays. Free forever — no upgrade required unless you hit the limits.
  2. Lovely Home+ — upgrade from your hub or choose Plus at signup. Pay £4.99/month or £44.99/year (yearly is best value). Stripe handles billing securely.
  3. Cancel Plus — use your account page or email support@lovely-home.co.uk. After a paid period ends, Plus features revert to Free limits unless you renew.
  4. When a hub ends — if you cancel entirely, the live hub is taken down and archived. Download a password-encrypted full backup from Settings while the hub is still up if you want photos and PDFs — our platform archive on cancel is guide JSON only.

Prices are as shown. VAT is not charged. Terms of service.
