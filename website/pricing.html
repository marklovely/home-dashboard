How billing works

  1. Sign up — pick a hub address and email. Stripe Checkout collects your card; you are not charged today. Stripe shows the first charge date before you confirm.
  2. Trial — 7 days to get the hub ready before your sitter arrives. The trial is for setup, not a complimentary stay. A wall tablet is optional.
  3. After the trial — your saved card is charged £9.99/month or £99/year (whichever you chose), unless you cancel first.
  4. Cancel — use your account page (we email a code to the signup address), or email support@lovely-home.co.uk with your hub address. Stop before the trial ends and pay nothing. After a paid period, cancel anytime; access continues until that period ends.
  5. When it ends — the live hub is taken down and archived. You can download a password-encrypted backup while the hub is still up. Photos and appliance PDFs are not inside that file.
