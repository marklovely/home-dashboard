Support

Home hub for your wall tablet — questions, setup tips, and how to reach us.

Lovely Home is a progressive web app (PWA) for wall tablets and browsers. It brings together a house guide, bin reminders, weather, Alexa routines, and owner setup tools for a single household.

Each paying household gets a private hub at {name}.lovely-hub.com — only people you invite can sign in. Start a 14-day free trial (see pricing) or explore the public demo hub first (username demo, password lovely-demo; fictional data, resets nightly).

For questions, feedback, or help with the product in general, email: support@lovely-home.co.uk

Common topics

  • Pricing & billing — Transparent monthly pricing after a 14-day trial. Card on file at signup; cancel before the trial ends to pay nothing.
  • Try before you buy — Open the public demo. Sign in with username demo and password lovely-demo (pre-filled on the sign-in page). Data resets overnight.
  • Installing on a tablet — Open your household hub URL in the browser, then use the browser’s “Add to Home Screen” (or Fully Kiosk start URL) so it launches fullscreen.
  • House sitter mode — A simpler home screen and optional screensaver for guests. Owner tools are hidden until you unlock with your owner PIN.
  • House guide — Topics with photos, edited by the owner. Content is stored on the household’s hub server.
  • Alexa routines — Requires Virtual Buttons (or compatible integration) configured by the household owner.
  • Not your hub? — This site describes the Lovely Home product. Your wall tablet URL and login details come from whoever manages your household — we cannot reset access for a private installation.
