Part of your home hub

House sitters get what they need — when they need it

Lovely Home is the digital home hub for your home. Schedule a stay for your sitter and they sign in on their phone — house guide, bins, weather, and home-access details that unlock on arrival day. Works for Trusted Housesitters, friends, family, and any invited guest.

What sitters see (and when)

Schedule each stay with their email and dates. They sign in with a one-time code — no app install.

  1. Two weeks before. They can read the house guide — heating, appliances, bins, and emergency contacts. Wi‑Fi, address, and lockbox stay hidden.
  2. During the stay. Home-access details unlock automatically.
  3. After checkout. Their login is removed the next day.

Free includes up to two scheduled stays; Lovely Home+ is unlimited. See how to set it up and the full home hub story.

Better than a message thread

  • One hub URL — updates you make appear for every future stay
  • Written for humans — photos, search, and clear categories in the house guide
  • Bin collection reminders so nothing is missed while you are away
  • Optional Alexa routine buttons for lights and heating (when you connect Virtual Buttons)
  • Private to your home — not a public listing or shared app

Pricing

Free forever — one home, two guides, two scheduled stays. Lovely Home+ — £4.99/month or £44.99/year for unlimited guides and stays. No hardware to buy; sitters use their own phone.
