One household, one hub

What's included

Everything below is in the plan. A few extras need an account or device you already own — we say so rather than burying it.

Ready when your hub is live

You fill in the household details during setup (about 30 minutes). Hosting, HTTPS, and sign-in are already there.

Capability Status You provide
Private hub at {name}.lovely-hub.com Included A short hub address at signup — it cannot be changed later
House guide and editor Included Your topics and photos; starter templates are there to edit
Appliance manuals Included PDF files you upload
Wi‑Fi QR with the Lovely Home mark Included Network name and password in setup
Guest / sitter mode and owner PIN Included A PIN you choose; owner tools stay locked until you unlock them
Scheduled sitter stays Included Email and dates for each stay; guide 7 days before, home-access details on sit dates, login removed after checkout
Emergency contacts Included Names and numbers you enter
Weather and 7-day outlook Included Location or postcode
Bin collection and reminders Included Your council collection dates
Screensaver and kiosk behaviour Included Optional — a wall tablet if you want kiosk mode (see Set it up)
Password-encrypted backup Included A password you choose. Photos and PDF manuals are not inside the file — re-upload those after a restore

Needs your own account or hardware

These work on the hub once you connect them. They are not a separate Lovely Home fee.

Capability Status You provide
Alexa routine buttons Your account A Virtual Buttons (or compatible) account, set up by the owner
Cameras on the hub Your network A go2rtc (or similar) gateway on your home network
My Day calendar Your calendar An ICS feed URL (for example an Apple or Google calendar subscribe link)

Not included

  • Wall tablet, mount, or kiosk hardware — optional, and you choose and buy those if you want them
  • Alexa speakers, smart bulbs, or other home devices
  • A Fully Kiosk licence, if you use that app
  • More than one household on a single subscription
